    You source your own parts. These are the best places to start.

    Never open anything plugged into the mains (power supplies, microwaves), CRT screens, or swollen and damaged lithium batteries. Not sure? Ask in the group first.

      Section 04Ask and test

      Ask around. Test first.

      Where to ask

      1. Repair shops. Mobile, TV, laptop and AC shops keep a bin of dead boards. Visit when it is quiet, say what you are building, ask what is in the dead pile.
      2. Home. The drawer of dead remotes, toys and keyboards. Ask before you take anything.
      3. College labs. Ask the lab in-charge for retired PCs and printers. Get permission first.
      4. Scrap dealers. Often sold by weight. Ask to pick your own parts, wear gloves, be patient. The good stuff hides in the pile.
      5. Other teams. Short on something? Swap with a nearby team.

      Test before you hoard

      1. Photograph first. Take a picture before you open anything. It saves you later.
      2. Motors. Touch the two wires to an AA battery for a second. A smooth spin is a keeper. Humming or stiff, skip it.
      3. Switches and wires. Multimeter on the beep (continuity) setting. A beep means the path is good.
      4. Read the label. 5V, 12V or 24V is printed on motors and boards. Write it on the bag.
      5. Leave the bad ones. Swollen, leaking or rusted capacitors and batteries stay behind.
      6. Cut wires long. Leave about 5 cm. Bag parts by type, not by device.

      CarryGloves, closed shoes, zip bags, a marker, a torch and your phone for filming. Screwdrivers wait until after the safety session. Do not shop: only buy safety, power or basic connectivity.

      Section 05Scrap Diary

      Film your scrap diary.

      Short clips from the hunt, vlog style, under a minute each. Walk into a scrap shop, open a dead gadget, test a part, say what it could become. Show the failures too.

      01

      The hunt

      Film the walk in. Show the shop, the pile, and you asking what they have.

      02

      The haul

      Lay it all out. Name each part out loud, even if you are guessing.

      03

      The teardown

      Open a safe device on camera and narrate what you find. Safety session first.

      04

      The test

      Multimeter on. Does it work? Say so, either way.

      05

      The idea

      Ten seconds to camera. What could this pile become?

Section 01Overview

Start with the scrap.
Not the idea.

Most hackathons start with an idea, then you buy the parts. Scrap Hack flips it. You find discarded electronics, learn what is inside, and build from what you recover.

Step 1

Find the components

Scrap shops, old toys, dead gadgets at home.

Step 2

Understand how they work

Test them with a multimeter. Learn what each part does.

Step 3

Explore possibilities

Match your parts to ideas. Stay flexible.

Step 4

Build something

Prototype, break it, fix it.

The theme

Tech for the environment, starting with what we throw away.

Scrap Hack sits under our food, agri and environment theme. Dead printers, radios and toys are full of motors, speakers and switches that still work. Instead of buying new parts, you give old ones a second life.

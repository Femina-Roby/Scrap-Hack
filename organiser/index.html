Who am I

i'm femina. i work as a project coordinator at tinkerhub foundation. most days, i spend my time talking to campus leads and wondering what is the next big thing we can do for these students.

i myself am a complete beginner when it comes to hardware (barely know what jumper wires are). and as someone with zero ball knowledge in hardware, why would i choose to conduct a hardware hackathon? good question. the literal answer is, why not. i wanted to do something of my own, but i had no idea. then came *drumroll* Arundhathi Krishna.

smooth transition into the next section ↓

Why Scrap Hack

so there i was, wanting to do something but nothing to do. so then, this powerhouse of ideas comes up to me and says, “Why don't you run Scrap Hack?” yessss! of course, i'm up for it, i'm for anything. naturally, it came under my ownership. i gotta admit, this idea belonged to *another drumroll* Shan (Space Manager, TinkerSpace Kochi).

the whole point of Scrap Hack is simple: get people familiarized with hardware. basic sensors, breadboards, microcontrollers, etc.

although, there was a catch. you'd have to source your components from scrap shops, broken electronics, and discarded items.
